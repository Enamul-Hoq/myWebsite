What I work on

Three problems I keep
coming back to

Lung cancer screening produces enormous volumes of imaging and enormous numbers of false alarms. Most of my work sits between those two facts.

Radiology

Making screening CT tractable

Whole-volume 3D training is expensive and hard to reproduce. I showed that frozen, native-resolution 2D embeddings reach comparable patient-level performance at a fraction of the compute — and behave the way radiologists actually read, slice by slice.

Pathology

Teaching models to describe tissue

Captioning a whole-slide image is easy to do badly and dangerous to do wrong. I ground generation in retrieved cases so the model describes what it can point to, and measured the drop in unsafe output.

Dissertation · 2022–2026

LCS-DINO

Foundation modelCTA100 · Slurm
WhatAn end-to-end pipeline that estimates lung cancer risk from screening CT at native resolution, from DICOM ingestion to a calibrated patient-level score.
HowSelf-supervised RAD-DINO embeddings over 50M+ slices, mean aggregation to the patient, isotonic calibration, and a fixed leakage-safe split with repeated imbalanced test draws at roughly screening prevalence.
ResultROC-AUC 0.817 and calibrated PR-AUC 0.705 at ~6% prevalence, with far lower compute than volumetric training. First-author paper in the Journal of Imaging Informatics in Medicine, 2025.

Accepted · MIDL 2026

Virtual-Eyes

Quality control16-bit
WhatA quality-control layer that decides which CT series are fit to enter a risk model at all, instead of letting preprocessing stay an undocumented footnote.
HowRule-driven, quantitative checks on a strict 512×512 16-bit grid — rejecting scouts, partial chest coverage and wrong fields of view — validated against how the downstream model actually behaves.
ResultMeasured, separable gains for generalist versus specialist models, and a preprocessing step that can be written into a protocol. Accepted at MIDL 2026, first author.

Mayo Clinic · 2025

Retrieval-guided pathology captioning

WSILLM
WhatContent-based retrieval and automatic captioning for histopathology whole-slide images, built so the caption stays tied to real evidence.
HowVision foundation-model embeddings for retrieval, a hybrid rule-based and LLM summarisation pipeline, and retrieval-guided generation that constrains what the model is allowed to claim.
ResultSemantic alignment above 0.82 cosine similarity with fewer unsafe and hallucinated captions. Top-ten poster at the Mayo Clinic AI Summit; accepted to Ital-IA 2026, first author.

Site lead · 2025–present

Federated LDCT across five institutions

NVIDIA FLAREPrivacy
WhatLung screening models trained jointly by UAMS, Moffitt, Vanderbilt, UCLA and the University of Pittsburgh, with no raw patient data leaving any site. I lead UAMS as Site 2.
HowNVIDIA FLARE for secure model updates, harmonised multi-site curation, leakage-safe cross-site splits, and evaluation protocols that run identically at every institution.
ResultBetter detection and lower projected follow-up workload than any single site could reach alone. The approach took three of five categories at the CMU–NVIDIA federated learning hackathon, 2026.

Winthrop P. Rockefeller Cancer Institute

Synthetic-phantom monitoring of a deployed AI

Post-marketIRB
WhatA way to answer the question nobody asks after purchase: is the commercial nodule-detection software still measuring the same thing it measured last year?
HowMATLAB phantom modelling that injects synthetic pulmonary nodules into real DICOM volumes, plus a purpose-built database and analysis workflow, under an IRB-approved prospective protocol.
ResultA reproducible, auditable method for continuous evaluation of clinical AI — the kind of surveillance good machine learning practice asks for and almost nobody has.

NeurIPS 2025 · co-author

Crop-guided diffusion for tissue synthesis

DiffusionAugmentation
WhatA generative model that synthesises heterogeneous tissue to enlarge histopathology training sets where real annotated examples are scarce.
HowSemantic and visual crop guidance, so generated regions stay consistent with the tissue context they are dropped into.
ResultPeer-reviewed acceptance at NeurIPS 2025, with a usable augmentation route for pathology models.
